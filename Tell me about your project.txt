    CTS Clearing 
    -> CTS clearing stands for Cheque Truncation System clearing. Introduced by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), it is an online, image-based cheque clearing process that speeds up realization.Instead of physically moving paper cheques between banks, a scanned electronic image of the cheque and its MICR data are securely transmitted to the clearing house for processing.

    What Happens in CTS?
    ->
        Customer deposits cheque.
        Bank scans cheque image.
        MICR, account details, amount, and image are captured.
        Data is sent electronically to the clearing house.
        Paying bank verifies the cheque.
        Cheque is accepted or rejected.
        Settlement is completed.
